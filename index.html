Data on the turnover of French wine in Japan reveals a complex and evolving situation. In 2024, exports of wines and spirits contributed 14 billion euros to France’s trade balance, with a turnover of 16.5 billion euros for wines and spirits. In 2025, France experienced a significant decline in wine imports, reflecting economic challenges and shifts in consumer preferences. French wines, particularly those from Bordeaux, enjoy an exceptional reputation in China, but competition from Australia and Chile is intensifying.

How the score is calculated

Example: China. Each criterion’s weight is divided by 5, then multiplied by the grade.

  • Market size[5% / 5] × 55%
  • Purchasing power[10% / 5] × 36%
  • Wine consumption[20% / 5] × 520%
  • Wine imports[20% / 5] × 520%
  • Regulations[25% / 5] × 210%
  • Logistics[20% / 5] × 416%

NextQuestion 3 · Choose your way in

Question 3

Choose your way in.
Japan.

1. Our way in

Local importer/distributor
+ direct-to-consumer online sales.

We would enter Japan through a local wine importer/distributor, while keeping control of our brand, subscription concept and online customer experience.

Our assumption

Japanese consumers have sufficient purchasing power and demand for imported wine to support a premium French wine discovery subscription, while a local partner can make market entry faster and easier.

3. The outsider question

Who takes us inside?

Who takes us inside the local network?

A Japanese wine importer/distributor specialised in French wines, ideally with experience in e-commerce and premium wines.

What do they know that we don’t?

They understand Japanese consumers, local wine distribution, import procedures, pricing, business culture and relationships with retailers and logistics providers.

Our role remains focused on French wine selection, brand identity, subscription experience and customer relationship.

4. Our 90-day thresholds

Scale, or stop and reassess.

Scale

  • ≥ 300paying subscribers
  • ≥ 25%make an additional purchase on the online shop
  • ≥ 60%subscriber retention

Stop / Reassess

  • < 100paying subscribers
  • < 10%make an additional purchase
  • < 35%subscriber retention

These thresholds allow us to test three essential things: customer acquisition, additional wine purchases and retention.

Final research question

Are Japanese consumers willing to pay regularly for a subscription specifically designed to discover different French wines?
